Print & production notes

Hand any of these to a printer with the following.

Trim size
85 × 55 mm (UK / international standard). US alternative: 3.5 × 2 in.
Bleed
Add 3 mm bleed on all sides → artboard 91 × 61 mm. Keep text 4 mm inside the trim.
Stock
Uncoated or soft-touch matte, 350–400 gsm. Matte suits the minimal look; avoid gloss.
Finish (optional)
Blind deboss or spot-matte on the name/monogram reads expensive on white without adding colour.
Colour
True black text — spec as rich black (e.g. C40 M30 Y30 K100) for depth, not flat K100.
Files
Supply vector PDF (outlined type) at 300 dpi. This HTML is the visual reference to hand a designer.
