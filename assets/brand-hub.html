Designing for the one-person businessOld Mutual · SME Product+

"When businesses understand their numbers, they stop surviving and start thriving."

The challenge

Old Mutual wanted a platform to help South African small-business owners — freelancers, vendors, roadside traders — understand their money, track performance and stay tax-compliant. Most tools are built for teams; this had to work for a team of one.

Five persona-driven journeys capturing income, expenses and performance; contextual learning moments (tax tips, VAT, funding); simple profit-and-cash-flow dashboards; and micro-education loops that taught owners about their business as they tracked it.
