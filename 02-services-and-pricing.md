# 02 · Services & Pricing

*Four productised offers that form a ladder: land with a low-risk diagnosis, expand into builds
and retainers. Prices are positioning bands — start at the lower end while you build case
studies, raise as proof stacks up.*

## Offer 1 — The Automation Opportunity Scan  *(your hook)*

**The promise your whole brand makes, productised.** You spend a short, structured period
inside the business, then hand back a ranked map of exactly what to automate, what each play is
worth, and how hard it is.

**What they get:**
- Interviews with 3–6 people across the messy processes.
- A process map of where time and money leak (failed payments, rekeying, manual reviews,
  chasing, reconciliation, compliance admin).
- A **ranked opportunity register**: each automation scored on annual £/R value, effort, and
  risk — a clear "do these three first" answer.
- A one-page executive summary + a 20–30 min readout.
- A fixed quote for the top play (→ leads straight into a Sprint).

**Why it sells:** it's low-risk, fast, and it makes the invisible visible. It also disqualifies
tyre-kickers and gives you a paid way to earn trust before a big build.

**Format:** 1–2 weeks · mostly remote, 1 onsite/virtual workshop day.

**Price band:**
- ZAR: **R25,000 – R60,000**
- GBP: **£1,500 – £3,500**
- *Credit the full fee back against a Sprint booked within 30 days.* (Removes all risk for the
  buyer, guarantees you the next sale.)

## Offer 2 — The Automation Sprint  *(the build)*

**Fixed-scope, fixed-price delivery of the single highest-value automation** from the Scan — a
working thing, in their environment, that they own.

**Examples of a Sprint deliverable:**
- A failed-payment recovery flow (retries, dunning, reporting) — *you've built this.*
- An AI intake / triage assistant for a law firm or claims team.
- A document-extraction + review pipeline (invoices, policies, contracts, statements).
- A reconciliation or month-end automation.
- An AML/KYC screening + audit-trail workflow — *you've built this.*
- An AI support/receptionist deflecting inbound volume — *you've built this (60% resolution).*

**What they get:** discovery → build → test → deploy → handover, with documentation and a short
training session. Optional 30-day support tail.

**Format:** 2–6 weeks depending on scope.

**Price band:**
- ZAR: **R80,000 – R350,000** per sprint
- GBP: **£5,000 – £20,000** per sprint
- Price by value delivered, not hours. If a play saves R1.5m/year, R250k is cheap.

## Offer 3 — The AI Enablement Retainer  *(ongoing)*

**You become their outsourced automation function.** A rolling relationship where you keep
finding and shipping automations, maintain what's live, and keep them current as AI moves.

**What they get:**
- A set monthly capacity for build + maintenance.
- A living opportunity backlog you keep re-scoring.
- Monitoring and improvements on shipped automations.
- A monthly "here's what we saved / shipped / should do next" report to their exec.

**Format:** 3-month minimum, monthly rolling after.

**Price band:**
- ZAR: **R35,000 – R120,000 / month**
- GBP: **£2,500 – £8,000 / month**
- Tiered (e.g. Lite / Growth / Embedded) by capacity.

---

## Offer 4 — Fractional Head of Automation  *(embedded leadership)*

**For a company going all-in.** You embed part-time as the person who owns their automation and
AI agenda — strategy, roadmap, vendor decisions, and hands-on delivery — without a full-time
executive hire.

**What they get:** 1–2 days/week of you owning the automation function end to end: strategy,
build oversight, team direction, exec reporting.

**Format:** 3–12 months, 1–2 days/week.

**Price band:**
- ZAR: **R60,000 – R180,000 / month**
- GBP: **£4,000 – £12,000 / month**

This is the premium tier and the most "Rihanna" positioning — not a vendor, an owner.

## Add-ons / smaller entry points (optional)

- **AI Readiness Workshop** — a half-day session for a leadership team: where AI actually helps
  *them*, demystified, with a shortlist of first moves. *R15,000 / £900.* Great top-of-funnel
  and speaking-adjacent.
- **Automation Teardown (content-led)** — you publicly teardown a common process in a sector and
  show the automated version. Free/marketing, but a killer lead magnet.

## Pricing principles (read before quoting)

1. **Anchor on value, not time.** Always translate the play into an annual £/R number first,
   then price a fraction of it. Your Scan gives you that number.
2. **Start low, climb fast.** Your first 2–3 engagements can sit at the bottom of each band to
   win logos and testimonials. Raise 20–40% per case study earned.
3. **Two currencies.** Quote ZAR to local clients, GBP/USD to UK/international (your bOnline and
   Emerald Path work gives you full credibility to bill internationally). International rates are
   your real market.
4. **Always ladder.** Every Scan proposal names the likely Sprint. Every Sprint close mentions
   the Retainer. Never deliver and disappear.
5. **Package the risk away.** The "Scan fee credited to the Sprint" mechanic makes the first
   "yes" almost free for the buyer and near-guarantees the second.
