What if I change my mind?

Cheap before you sign. Expensive after. If you change your mind after sign-off, I re-open the ticket. We change the scenarios. I re-quote the points. Nothing sneaks through.

Who decides what's next?

You. I suggest an order every Friday. You can overrule. The Jira board is the truth.

How do I check on you without asking?

Three places. The Jira board. The staging site. The Friday note. Zero meetings is fine.

How do you handle bad news?

The moment I know. Not Friday. A Slack message with a proposed fix, framed as yes or no.

How do I trust the AI?

Every AI line points back to its source. Anything the AI isn't sure about is held for you to review. Same pattern I built at Emerald Path.

Do you write tests?

Yes. Three kinds. A failing test names the requirement it broke.

  • 1. Cucumber + Playwright for user flows.
  • 2. Vitest for small units.
  • 3. Regression tests from last quarter's bugs.

A test is never turned off to pass. A flake is checked once, not skipped.

How many agents work on a ticket?

Eight. Each has one job and a small toolset. The cheap AI can't secretly use the expensive one.

Why different AIs for different jobs?

Opus plans and reviews. Sonnet builds. Haiku runs the loop. Cheap where cheap works. Expensive where it has to.

Who reviews the AI's code?

A different AI to the one that wrote it. Different blind spots. If it finds a real bug, the merge is blocked.

What if you disappear tomorrow?

Three things stay behind. The agents in your repo. The .feature files. Jira has the whole history. Another dev can pick it up.

Who owns the code?

You. From day one. Your repo, your license. The agents are plain text files. Edit them without me.

Can the agents run without you?

Yes. They fire on Jira and GitHub events. Your team runs them. That's the point.

How much of your time do I need?

About 45 minutes a week. Sign tickets on Monday (2-5 min each). Friday note (90 seconds). A 30-min call if you want. Zero meetings is also fine.

What do you need to buy?

A Jira seat (~$8/mo). A Claude API key (you set the cap). GitHub. That's it. Agents run on your bill, so you see every token.
