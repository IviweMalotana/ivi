How I scope, and where I've done it before.

How do you take an idea from me?

Whatever format you use: voice note, Slack thread, whiteboard photo, one-line email. The intake agent transcribes and OCRs it, extracts what/why/open-questions into a scratch doc. The ticket-writer agent then drafts a Jira story from it. You are not writing clean specs for me to work.

What do you need me to sign?

One Jira ticket per unit of work, with a .feature file attached. Cucumber, BDD, Gherkin. One Scenario per requirement, tagged @req-N. You read four to six bullets and reply "signed off" or edit them. Two to five minutes of your time.

What happens if I change my mind mid-build?

Cheap before the Gherkin is signed. Expensive after. If a change lands after sign-off, I re-open the ticket, we amend the scenarios together, I re-quote the points. Nothing is silently absorbed.

Who decides what goes next?

You do. I recommend an order every week with reasoning. You can always overrule. The Jira backlog is the source of truth. The "Next" line in the Friday note is my recommendation, not a decision.
