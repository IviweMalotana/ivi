# 05 · The 90-Day Launch Plan

*How to actually go live. Three phases: Build the base (Weeks 1–3), Go public (Weeks 4–7),
Fill the pipeline & sell (Weeks 8–12). Designed to be run alongside a full-time job — a few
focused hours a week, front-loaded.*

## Phase 1 — Build the base (Weeks 1–3)

The goal: a small, sharp footprint that makes you look inevitable, not "just starting."

**Week 1 — Identity & assets**
- [ ] Finalise the wordmark (clean set of your name — see `01-positioning-and-strategy.md §4`).
- [ ] Print business cards from `assets/business-cards.html` (pick one design; see notes in that
  file for print specs).
- [ ] Set up `ivi@ivimalotana.com`.
- [ ] Create a simple brand asset folder (logo, colours, headshot, one-pager PDF).

**Week 2 — The one-page site**
- [ ] Build a clean one-pager (upgrade from the Canva site). Structure:
  1. **Hero:** name + "AI & Automation Specialist" + the signature line *"See the opportunity.
     Automate the outcome."* + one line of positioning + a "Book an Opportunity Scan" button.
  2. **The problem** (3 lines: money & time leaking in manual process).
  3. **What I do** — the offer ladder (Scan → Sprint → Retainer).
  4. **Proof wall** — the numbers from `04-proof-case-studies.md`.
  5. **Sectors** — Law · Finance · Insurance, one line each (from the playbooks).
  6. **About** — engineer-operator story.
  7. **CTA** — book a call (Calendly/Cal.com link).
- [ ] Use `assets/one-pager.html` as the content source — the site is the same copy, expanded.

**Week 3 — LinkedIn overhaul + proof**
- [ ] Rewrite your LinkedIn using `06-content-and-outreach.md` (headline, about, featured).
- [ ] Publish the 3 core case studies (finance, compliance/Emerald Path, AI support) as LinkedIn
  articles or posts — this is your portfolio, working while you sleep.
- [ ] Add "AI & Automation Specialist" to your headline and turn on "Open to / Providing
  services."
- [ ] Get 2–3 short testimonials from past colleagues/managers (even informal).

**End of Phase 1 = launch-ready.** Site live, cards in hand, LinkedIn reflects the brand, proof
published.

## Phase 2 — Go public (Weeks 4–7)

The goal: announce, then show up consistently so the market starts to associate your name with
"automation."

**Week 4 — The launch post**
- [ ] Publish your announcement post (template in `06`). Not "I'm looking for work" — "Here's the
  thing I now do, and here's the proof." Pin it.
- [ ] Personally message ~30 people in your network with a warm, specific note (template in `06`).
- [ ] Update all bios (LinkedIn, X, IG, email signature) to the new positioning.

**Weeks 5–7 — Content rhythm (this is the engine)**
- [ ] Post **2× / week** on LinkedIn following the content calendar in `06`. Rotate:
  - a **teardown** (pick a common process in law/finance/insurance, show the automated version),
  - a **proof** post (a case study, reframed),
  - a **POV** post (a strong opinion on AI/automation in one of your sectors).
- [ ] Publish one **"Automation Teardown"** long-form piece — your signature content format. It
  doubles as a lead magnet.
- [ ] Start a simple lead tracker (spreadsheet): name, company, sector, source, stage, next step.

**End of Phase 2 = visible.** You've announced, you're posting consistently, inbound curiosity
starts.

---

## Phase 3 — Fill the pipeline & sell (Weeks 8–12)

The goal: convert attention into paid Scans, and Scans into Sprints.

**Weeks 8–10 — Outreach**
- [ ] Build a target list of **30–50 companies** in your lead sector (use the ICP in `01 §5`).
- [ ] Send **10 warm/cold outreach messages a week** (scripts in `06`) — lead with the free
  teardown or a specific observation, not a pitch.
- [ ] Offer **2–3 free "mini-scans"** (a 45-min call + a one-page opportunity sketch) to warm
  contacts to generate your first testimonials and refine the paid Scan.
- [ ] Line up **1 speaking / workshop slot** — a webinar, a meetup, an "AI for [sector]" session.
  Repurpose the AI Readiness Workshop.

**Weeks 11–12 — Convert**
- [ ] Turn mini-scans and inbound into **paid Opportunity Scans**. Use the fixed-fee, credited-
  to-Sprint mechanic to make the "yes" easy.
- [ ] Deliver your first paid Scan → present the ranked opportunity register → quote the Sprint.
- [ ] Ask every happy contact for **one referral and one testimonial**.
- [ ] Review: which sector responded best? Double down there next quarter.

**End of 90 days target:**
- Live site + brand.
- 3 published case studies.
- ~16–24 content posts shipped.
- 30–50 outreach touches.
- 10+ qualified conversations.
- 1–2 paid Scans delivered, ≥1 Sprint quoted or won.

## The weekly cadence (sustainable, alongside a job)

| Day | ~Time | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | 30 min | Write + schedule the week's 2 posts |
| Tue–Thu | 20 min/day | 10 outreach touches + reply to comments/DMs |
| Fri | 45 min | Pipeline review, follow-ups, book calls |
| Weekend (optional) | 1–2 hrs | One long-form piece or deliver Scan work |

**Rule:** consistency beats intensity. Two posts and ten touches a week for twelve weeks will
outperform one heroic burst.

---

## Metrics to watch (keep it simple)

- **Leading:** posts shipped, outreach sent, calls booked.
- **Lagging:** Scans sold, Sprints won, revenue, referrals.
- **Brand:** profile views, inbound DMs, "I saw your post about…" mentions.

Track the leading metrics weekly; they're the ones you control.
