## 3. Content calendar — the first 4 weeks (2 posts/week)

*Three rotating formats: **Teardown** (show a manual process → the automated version), **Proof**
(a case study reframed), **POV** (a strong, useful opinion).*

| Week | Post 1 | Post 2 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Launch post** (above) | **Proof:** the 7× billing story (Case Study 1) |
| 2 | **Teardown:** "How a law firm's client intake *actually* runs — and the 20-minute automated version" | **POV:** "Most 'AI strategies' fail because they start with the tool, not the leak" |
| 3 | **Proof:** Emerald Path — automating a whole accountancy back office (Case Study 2) | **Teardown:** "Where insurers are still paying people to retype documents" |
| 4 | **POV:** "AI should propose. Humans should approve. Here's why that pattern wins in regulated work." | **Proof:** 60% chatbot resolution (Case Study 3) + soft CTA to book a Scan |

**Reusable hooks (first lines that stop the scroll):**
- "Your business is leaking money in a place you can't see. Here's how to find it."
- "A [law firm / insurer / finance team] just told me they had 'no manual work.' Here's what I
  found in an hour."
- "Everyone's talking about AI. Almost nobody's talking about *where it actually pays for
  itself.*"
- "I scaled a company's billing 7× without adding a single person. Here's the boring truth of
  how."
- "'Ivi, we can't automate this — it needs human intervention.' Six weeks later that accountant
  had six extra hours a week. Here's what we did."

**The signature format — "Automation Teardown":** pick one common process in a sector, break down
each manual step and what it costs, then show the automated version and the annual saving. Ship
one a month. It's your lead magnet and your proof-of-thinking in one.

## 4. Outreach templates

### (a) Warm network message (Week 4)

> Hey [Name] — quick one. I've started doing something independently that I think is right up
> [Company / your world]'s street: I find where a business is losing time and money in manual
> processes and build the AI/automation to fix it (I did this for payments & fraud at a UK
> fintech, and built a whole compliance-automation platform).
>
> Not pitching you — but if [a manual process you know they have] is still eating hours over
> there, I'd happily do a free 45-minute teardown and show you what I'd automate first. Worth a
> chat?

### (b) Cold outreach — finance/CFO

> Hi [Name], I'll be brief. I rebuild the parts of finance operations that quietly lose money —
> failed payments, collections, reconciliation, fraud. At [fintech] I scaled billing 7× and stood
> up their first fraud & AML process.
>
> I run a short, fixed-fee "Opportunity Scan": I map where [Company] is leaking and rank exactly
> what to automate first, with the £ value attached — and I credit the fee against the build if
> you go ahead. Open to a 20-minute call to see if it's worth it?

### (c) Cold outreach — law firm / managing partner

> Hi [Name], how many billable hours a week do your fee-earners lose to client intake, KYC/AML
> admin and chasing invoices? I build the automation that hands those hours back — I've built
> live AML screening, auto-derived deadline tracking and document pipelines before.
>
> I'd offer a short, fixed-fee scan of where [Firm] is losing time, ranked by value. Worth 20
> minutes to explore?

### (d) Cold outreach — insurance / Head of Claims/COO

> Hi [Name], two questions that usually find money in a claims operation: where are you paying
> people to retype what's already on a document, and where are you paying out on claims a second
> look would've caught? Both are automatable and I've built the pieces.
>
> I run a short, fixed-fee Opportunity Scan that ranks exactly what to automate first, with the
> numbers. Could I show you how it'd work for [Company]?

### (e) Follow-up (if no reply, 4–5 days later)

> Hi [Name] — floating this back up in case it got buried. Even if the timing's not right, I put
> together a 2-minute teardown of [a process in their sector] that shows the kind of leak I mean
> — happy to send it over, no strings. Want it?

## 6. Two rules for all content & outreach

1. **Lead with their leak, not your service.** Every message opens with a problem they feel, not
   a description of you.
2. **Always offer the low-risk next step** — the free teardown or the fixed-fee, credited Scan.
   Never ask for a big commitment as the first move. Make the first "yes" almost free.
