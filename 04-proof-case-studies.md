## Case Study 1 — Scaling a fintech's payments & billing engine 7×

**Sector:** Finance / Fintech (bOnline, UK)
**Role:** Product Owner — payments, billing, fraud & compliance

**Context.** A UK fintech needed its billing and payments to support rapid customer growth
without a matching rise in operational cost or failed revenue.

**Problem.** Failed and retried payments, direct-debit failures, and inconsistent collections
were leaking revenue. There was no structured fraud or AML process. Growth was outpacing the
machine that collected the money.

**What Ivi did.**
- Designed and built the **direct-debit failure management system** end to end — retries,
  failure handling and downstream financial processes — using APIs and webhooks.
- Built the full **credit-control communication funnel** — customer journeys, messaging logic
  and escalation paths to recover receivables and cut bad debt.
- Led **checkout and recurring card payment** integrations for secure, compliant flows.
- Introduced the company's **first fraud detection, AML and governance framework**, including a
  third-party fraud system and customer due-diligence process.

**Result.** **Billing capacity scaled 7×**, supporting rapid growth with no added operational
overhead — and fraud/compliance exposure reduced from a standing start.

**Why it matters to you.** If revenue is quietly leaking through failed payments, weak
collections or reactive fraud handling, this is the exact machine I've built before.

## Case Study 2 — Emerald Path: automating an entire accountancy back office

**Sector:** Accountancy / Compliance / RegTech
**Role:** Founder-builder (commercialised as a 50/50 venture)

**Context.** UK accountancy practices spend enormous manual effort turning a client's books into
statutory accounts, tax returns and compliance evidence — repetitive, deadline-bound,
error-sensitive work.

**Problem.** The year-end pipeline (trial balance → adjustments → statutory accounts → tax →
filing) plus AML/KYC and deadline tracking is slow and manual, and mistakes carry regulatory and
financial risk.

**What Ivi did.** Designed and built **Emerald Path**, a multi-tenant, white-label platform that
runs the whole pipeline:
- Live **Companies House** onboarding, **Xero/Sage** trial-balance ingestion.
- **AI-proposed journal adjustments** with rationale and citations — *human approves every one.*
- Deterministic engines for **FRS-102 statutory accounts, iXBRL, Corporation Tax, VAT and
  Self-Assessment**, guarded by a **253-test regression suite**.
- Live **AML** (identity, PEP, sanctions, adverse-media) screening with a full audit trail.
- Auto-derived **statutory deadlines**, filings register, and per-firm data isolation with
  disaster recovery and monitoring.

**Result.** A working end-to-end platform where a firm goes from raw books to filing-ready
accounts and tax with AI doing the heavy lifting and humans staying in control — verified across
real engagements.

**Why it matters to you.** Any business built on documents, deadlines and compliance — law
firms, insurers, finance teams — has the same shape of problem. I've built the reference solution.
